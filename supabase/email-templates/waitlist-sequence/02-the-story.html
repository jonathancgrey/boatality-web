A note from Jonathan — the real reason Boatality exists, and why you're already part of it.

Hey — Jonathan here.

I built Boatality because I kept running into the same problem.

I'd find this incredible fishing channel — three guys posting the realest content about deep-sea trips, the failures, the wins, the stupid jokes that only make sense on the water. Hundreds of hours of work. Maybe a few thousand views. Then I'd find a sailing channel with this beautiful documentation of life aboard, the kind of thing that should be teaching people how to actually live differently. Smaller audience than a viral video from a week ago.

And I couldn't stop thinking about why.

"The answer wasn't that the content wasn't good enough. It's that the platforms weren't built for boat people."

YouTube wants 10-minute engagement metrics. Instagram wants 9-second clips. Neither of them understand that boat content — real boat content — builds slowly. It's about trust, community, and people who want to actually learn something.

So we're building the platform that should have existed already. For the creators who are making the stuff that matters to people who love the water.

You're not just early. You're the reason this exists.

See you on the water,
Jonathan
Founder, Boatality · jonathan@boatality.com
